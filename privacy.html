Privacy Policy — Linear Transform: y = Ax

Effective date: November 14, 2026

English

This privacy policy applies to the iPad app Linear Transform: y = Ax (the “App”), developed by H. Zhang, Gunma University, Japan.

  • No data collection. The App does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information or usage data.
  • No accounts. The App does not require sign-in or registration.
  • No network access. The App works fully offline and does not connect to any server.
  • No tracking, analytics or advertising. The App contains no third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising or tracking technologies.
  • On-device only. Matrices, vectors and settings you enter exist only in the App’s memory on your device while you use it.
  • Children. Because no data is collected, no data is collected from children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact: H. Zhang — h-zhang@gunma-u.ac.jp
